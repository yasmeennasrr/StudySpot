# Venue photos

The 15 demo places in `venues.json` are made up, so their photos are free stock
photos that show the kind of place, not the place itself. Each `photoUrl` links to
the photo on Unsplash's image server (`images.unsplash.com`).

All photos are under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to
use, including in apps, with no permission or payment needed. Credit is not
required, but we give it here. Unsplash+ (paid) photos are not used.

To change a photo, replace the link in `venues.json` (https only, at most 500
characters) and run `npm run seed` again; the seed updates existing places.
